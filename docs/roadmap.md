# Folio roadmap — shipped release
Status: AGREED by Factory/Claude Opus 5.5 and Grok 4.7, incorporating their acceptance corrections (see docs/reviews/*-agreement.md). All eleven items shipped and verified. See [release verification](release-verification.md) and [final review resolutions](reviews/final-resolutions.md).
Scope: ship every item below now. Preserve the existing green identity, composition donut, real bundled logos, concise copy, dismiss/restore, and exact approval. All automated merchant writes remain controlled tests. Personal subscription changes are explicitly recorded by the user, never inferred from a sandbox result.

## 1. Versioned subscription lifecycle and billing truth
Migrate old workspace records on load without losing fields. Track charge history, active/cancel-pending/cancelled status, end date and user-recorded change history. Compute the next renewal from the billing anchor; never invent a posted charge when a date passes. Month-end/leap-year logic is consistent in table, calendar and overview. Cancel-pending stays in current cost until its end date. No renewal is projected on or after the end date. Once the end date passes, it leaves monthly cost and upcoming charges. Savings are labeled projected or user-recorded monthly reductions, never bank-verified or realized money without transaction evidence.
Acceptance: old records load; past renewals roll forward; cancelled rows disappear from upcoming charges; pending cancellation and month boundaries are tested. A cancel-pending end date equal to the next renewal excludes that renewal.

## 2. Reliable, reviewable card-statement import
Support common bank header aliases plus manual column mapping; explicit ambiguous-date format and debit/credit sign conventions. Recognize refunds/credits without abs() treating them as spending. Normalize known merchant descriptors conservatively, populate official domain/category/logo, and retain unknown names without inventing identity. Infer monthly/annual recurrence from charge intervals; single charges are Possible and unselected, recurring candidates Likely. Detect unsupported weekly/quarterly patterns and flag them rather than silently calling them monthly. Review charge history and edit name, plan, domain, category, amount, cycle and next date. Merge into an existing subscription only through a reviewed match; preserve charge history and flag price changes or charges after cancellation. Show rows read, skipped reasons (non-USD counted by currency), and truncation/candidate limits explicitly.
Acceptance: representative synthetic Chase/Amex/Capital One/generic headers, date ambiguity, sign conventions, refund, descriptor variation, one-off, repeated charge, changed price and merge cases pass. No silent loss or auto-adding all merchants.

## 3. Receipt discovery that preserves source facts
Parse multipart .eml, base64/quoted-printable and HTML to readable text with a maintained parser; support multiple receipt files. Keep concise source excerpts and mark inferred fields for review; reuse import duplicate matching. Do not execute email instructions. Gmail remains visibly unavailable until its credentials are supplied, with receipt import working.
Acceptance: multipart/HTML/encoded fixtures and multi-file import work; unknown prices/dates require review rather than fabricated certainty.

## 4. Consistent usage evidence and decisions
Typed usage metrics (active days, uses, quota, other) with units and observation dates; cost per use only when meaningful. Quick check-in captures whether user would renew and personal/shared/background value. One shared evidence selector powers badges, verdicts and cited recommendations; same-day newer entries win. Browser visits remain partial, neutral evidence, never enough alone for cancel/downgrade. Old evidence stays visible and drops out of actionable savings after 60 days. Lower-plan, annual and alternative verdicts show dollar savings only from a fresh confirmed offer; otherwise they say price unconfirmed and are excluded from totals and proposals. Every active subscription has a concise verdict: keep, needs evidence, review cancellation, lower plan, annual or alternative. Price increases/overlapping categories are review signals, not automatic cancellation or duplicate-service claims.
Persist inspection draft in Neon with TTL and session binding; allow review/edit before save and evidence deletion. Warn briefly before opening third-party cloud browser.
Acceptance: exact deciding source/date/confidence are shown; later receipts cannot masquerade as usage; check-in, stale, partial, same-day and shared-value cases are tested; previewed draft saves across instances.

## 5. Persistent alternatives and confirmed plan prices
Save research summary, linked sources, checkedAt and structured candidate plans (name, amount, cycle, source URL, exact quote, lost capability/migration effort). Reject model-extracted prices lacking a verbatim supporting quote/amount in returned source text; label extracted offers as needing user confirmation. Users can confirm a target offer or enter merchant terms themselves with provenance. Price freshness is 30 days. No automatic Exa calls on page loads. Real downgrade/annual/switch proposals use a fresh confirmed target, never $9/20%/$5 placeholders. Demo illustrative targets are labeled and still must be logically valid.
Acceptance: research survives closing/reload; annual only on monthly with lower equivalent confirmed price; downgrade is cheaper; unsupported/stale offers cannot silently become an approved target; dismissals respected.

## 6. Renewal decisions and useful return visits
Replace repeated annual-savings spotlight with a short renewal decision queue. Show potential savings once, user-recorded monthly reductions separately, and demo-applied reductions labeled Demo. Show next-7-day renewals, the largest item needing evidence and refreshed/stale decisions; Keep hides a decision until the next billing cycle, Review opens details. Brief since-last-visit summary includes recorded changes and imported price changes, and labels projected renewals as unconfirmed. Provide private ICS renewal calendar with a revocable random token, all-day dates and advance alarms. Recovery/calendar tokens use at least 128 random bits and are stored hashed; calendar is no-store, contains only names, amounts and dates, and excludes evidence/notes and cancelled subscriptions. No email, unsolicited messages or background crawls.
Acceptance: timezone-safe rolling dates, kept decisions reappear next cycle, cancelled charges disappear, token rotation invalidates old calendar URL; ICS validates with fixtures. Do not claim Google/Apple import tested unless actually tested.

## 7. Exact proposals with a complete lifecycle
Filter invalid actions: no more-expensive downgrade, no annual-to-annual, migration only when data exists. Proposals contain exact target, price, cycle, effective date, consequence and version of originating subscription/offer. End-of-period default for cancel/downgrade, with explicit approved date. Discard actually withdraws a proposal; stale proposals expire after 24h; one running action per subscription. Failed/interrupted runs are re-prepared with fresh approval, never replayed automatically. Persist progress and continue polling activity when dialog closes, including after reload. Sensitive/changed terms stop the run.
Acceptance: duplicate approval, changed subscription/offer, concurrent actions, discard, expiry and interrupted run tested.

## 8. Independent controlled merchant execution
Replace page.setContent target-state fixture with hosted controlled merchant pages and merchant records in Neon, reached by Kernel navigation. Target offers live in merchant state before action; the agent reads visible controls and chooses bounded navigation/clicks. Use at least two flow variants across all four action types (plan selection, nested menu, retention/decoy choices, confirmation). Check merchant state independently server-side before marking success, with export/import content equality for migration. Merchant state changes only through hosted merchant handlers authorized by a per-run merchant token. Neither the Folio action API nor the agent can write merchant state directly; the agent only operates visible controls. Verify status, plan, price, cycle and effective date against the approved proposal. Store a receipt and permitted screenshot/verification artifact; never expose merchant access tokens in workspace exports.
A completed demo action offers an explicit Apply to demo ledger step; no automatic changes to tracked personal records. Personal sandbox results remain tests.
Acceptance: eight controlled runs (four kinds x two variants) match approved server-side state. Mismatched price/target fails closed. A run that never completes the merchant UI flow cannot pass; end-of-period changes remain scheduled, not immediate. Final demo apply is idempotent, updates cost/calendar/recommendations and supersedes obsolete proposals.

## 9. Personal follow-through and recorded outcomes
For personal subscriptions provide merchant site/manage link only when known or sourced from merchant official domain; otherwise require user-supplied link rather than inventing. Let user record a change performed in their own browser (cancel/end date or plan/price/cycle/renewal), optionally paste confirmation text for editable date extraction. Cancellation becomes pending until end date. Log before/after terms and projected monthly reduction separately from sandbox activity. Later imported charges after the recorded end date are flagged.
Acceptance: record/correct manual outcome, future cancellation end date and post-cancellation charge warning work; sandbox completion cannot report real savings. Recorded personal cancellation appears in its own recorded total; sandbox tests never do.

## 10. Workspace ownership and recovery
Full versioned JSON export/import with validation, explicit replace/merge choice or clearly communicated replace, preserving subscriptions, evidence, saved research, outcomes and dismissals. Omit all credentials, session/browser URLs, merchant tokens and recovery secrets. Imported actions become historical records, never executable approvals. One-time displayed random recovery code stored hashed, rotate/revoke, restore on another device; shared DB rate limit for recovery and expensive endpoints. Delete workspace and related sessions/tokens/merchant records, close live inspection, clear cookie, reject deletion during running writes. Brief data-flow disclosure of Neon, Gateway, Exa and Kernel.
Acceptance: backup round-trip, malicious/oversized backup rejection, token hashing/rotation/isolation, no executable restored approvals, delete cleanup and API limits tested.

## 11. Task-focused interface and contextual assistant
Primary Add data opens CSV/receipts choices; provider configuration lives under Help/connection details. Easy Demo/Personal choice; personal data never silently mixed into demo estimates. Move browser evidence import to Usage, keep all actual category filters, correct sort affordances and remove hard-coded person initials. Compact mobile subscription rows/cards expose cost, renewal and usage without horizontal scrolling, and assistant cannot cover actions. Preserve donut, reduce repeated explanatory copy. Chat uses bounded recent conversation, current evidence/research/recorded outcomes and safe internal links to relevant subscriptions; chat never executes writes.
Acceptance: desktop and 390px full task flows, keyboard/overlay/error states; first subscription within two phone screens where practical; assistant follow-up retains context and shows only safe links.

## Explicit exclusions from this release
Real-account automated writes; bank connectors; activating Gmail without credentials; identity-provider login; email/push reminders; durable distributed job queue/automatic merchant replay; multi-currency billing and new billing cadence support; Chrome Store publication. No speculative known bundle claims. Renewals stay forecasts, not invented bank transactions.

## Agreed acceptance locks
- Forecast roll-forward never creates charge history; only imported or user-recorded transactions do.
- Missing or partial browser evidence means needs evidence and no savings. A deciding current positive-renewal/shared/background-value check-in means keep. Deleting/replacing evidence recomputes verdicts. Specific change verdicts require relevant deciding evidence and, except cancellation, a fresh confirmed target.
- Personal potential dollars require confirmed offer deltas; recorded reductions require explicit personal outcomes. Demonstration deltas stay labeled and logically valid. Dismissed suggestions leave both savings totals and renewal decisions.
- Every skipped statement row has a reason; unknown currencies never become USD silently.
- Keep decisions identify the covered renewal date; last-opened is stored for return summaries.
- Controlled cancellation/downgrade remains pending/scheduled until its approved effective date. A downgrade is a scheduled plan change, not an account cancellation. Independent verification checks that date. Demo application requires both a Demo workspace and a Demo-source subscription. Confirmation extraction is an editable suggestion. Personal inspection stores only user-saved evidence, never screenshots.
- Migration requires an export-bearing fixture or explicit user declaration of data to move, never just a name/category assumption.
- All eight merchant tests run; distinct labels/decoys prohibit one hardcoded path from satisfying both variants.
- Demo/Personal labels match current mode; the 390px assistant never covers essential data or actions.
